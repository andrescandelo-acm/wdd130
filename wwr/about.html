Experience the ultimate rush with our guided river excursions. Whether you are a beginner looking for family fun or a thrill-seeker aiming for Class V rapids, our licensed team ensures safety and unforgettable memories on every trip.

History

Founded with a passion for river running, our company started with just two rafts and a desire to share thrilling outdoor expeditions. Over the years, we have guided thousands of explorers down beautiful canyons and roaring waters.

Our commitment has always focused on river conservation, rigorous guide safety certifications, and providing every participant with an authentic, safe, and exhilarating wilderness adventure.
